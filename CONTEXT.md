# Aisomedo Social Publishing

This context manages preparation, review, scheduling, and publication of media to Instagram.

## Language

**Paylaşım Paketi**:
Ordered media, caption, and rendered publication material that move through one publication lifecycle. The current package is called the Aktif Paket.
_Avoid_: Son görev içerik klasörü, Dojo Gönderisi

**Yayın Planı**:
The recurring biweekly rule that determines when publication work becomes due.
_Avoid_: Yayın Zamanı, tek seferlik plan

**Yayın Zamanı**:
One due occurrence of the Yayın Planı. It may be skipped or rescheduled without changing the plan.
_Avoid_: Yayın Planı

**Yayın İncelemesi**:
A pending decision over one immutable rendered revision and its caption. Approval, skipping, and rescheduling are distinct outcomes of the same review.
_Avoid_: Onay İsteği

**Tamamlanmış Paket**:
A Paylaşım Paketi whose Reel publication has been confirmed by Instagram. Skipped, rescheduled, failed, or cancelled packages are not completed.
_Avoid_: Atlanmış Paket, yayınlanmamış arşiv

**Video Bölüm Seçimi**:
The time intervals of a video retained in the montage. An empty selection means the entire video is retained, not that the video is excluded from the package.
_Avoid_: Kesilecek bölümler, çıkarılacak bölgeler
