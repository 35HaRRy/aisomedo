# Dojo Social Publishing

This context manages preparation, review, scheduling, and publication of dojo media to Instagram.

## Language

**Dojo Paylaşım Paketi**:
Ordered dojo media, caption, and rendered publication material that move through one publication lifecycle. The current package is called the Aktif Paket.
_Avoid_: Son görev içerik klasörü, Dojo Gönderisi

**Dojo Yayın Planı**:
The recurring biweekly rule that determines when dojo publication work becomes due.
_Avoid_: Yayın Zamanı, tek seferlik plan

**Yayın Zamanı**:
One due occurrence of the Dojo Yayın Planı. It may be skipped or rescheduled without changing the plan.
_Avoid_: Dojo Yayın Planı

**Yayın İncelemesi**:
A pending decision over one immutable rendered revision and its caption. Approval, skipping, and rescheduling are distinct outcomes of the same review.
_Avoid_: Onay İsteği

**Tamamlanmış Paket**:
A Dojo Paylaşım Paketi whose Reel publication has been confirmed by Instagram. Skipped, rescheduled, failed, or cancelled packages are not completed.
_Avoid_: Atlanmış Paket, yayınlanmamış arşiv
