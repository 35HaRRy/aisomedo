## Platform

## Product Purpose

Dojo Yayıncılık is the existing Turkish-language publishing workspace. This
redesign changes its appearance, not its functionality.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve dashboard, current package/media editing and uploads, publication
review, completed packages, activity, settings, browser pairing, and setup.
Keep existing validation, confirmations, permissions, and live updates.
Use the existing React application and installed dependencies.
